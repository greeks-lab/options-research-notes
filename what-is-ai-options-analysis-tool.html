What Is an AI Options Analysis Tool (and What It Is Not)

Audience: English-speaking investors learning options research tools

Grounded on: alphagbm.com/en · options · pricing · skills

Publisher: Greeks Lab (greeks-lab) — educational notes about public AlphaGBM product language

Status: Publish-ready · 2026-10-27 (Asia/Shanghai)

Short answer

An AI options analysis tool is software that helps you research listed equity options—volatility, strategy structures, payoff profiles, and risk factors—using market data and model-assisted summaries. It is a research workbench, not a broker, signal service, or money manager.

AlphaGBM frames itself publicly as an AI multi-asset investment research workbench (stocks, options, and related research tools). Its English options page invites you to explore volatility, strategies, and risk, and to register free for a daily research allowance shared across stocks and options. Paid plans add more research credits—compare them only on live Pricing. The product does not sell trade signals, auto-execute trades, or promise returns. Scores and candidates on research surfaces are for information only—not buy/sell instructions.

How public AlphaGBM pages describe the tool

  • Homepage (/en): options strategies candidates are ranked by opportunity score within each strategy, not a whole-market scan; scores are not win probabilities and are not ranked across strategies; scan time does not make quotes live.
  • Options (/en/options): “Explore volatility, strategies and risk. Register free to use a daily research allowance shared across stocks and options.”
  • Pricing (/en/pricing): Free, Plus, Pro, and one-time research credit packs; stocks and options share research credits; Alpha Agent requires a paid subscription. Dollar amounts and exact credit counts come from the current subscription configuration at checkout—do not trust third-party copies of prices.
  • Skills (/en/skills): research workflows include Options Strategies (compare candidates and score components, with funding, expiry, and assignment risks). Some focused tools are labeled Reference or release preview—treat those as method aids, not proofs of live trading features.

What an AI options analysis tool is *not*

Claim to avoid Why
“Score = win rate” Site text: scores are not win probabilities
“Whole-market real-time scan” Site text: not a whole-market scan; quotes retain data dates
Broker / auto-trade / managed money Research workbench positioning; no return guarantees
Delta = assignment probability Educational misuse—Delta is a sensitivity, not an assignment odds meter

Conditions and limits

  • Options involve leverage. Buyers can lose the full premium paid. Sellers can face losses larger than the premium received.
  • Research outputs depend on quote quality, timestamps, and model assumptions. Always check the displayed quote timestamps on the page.
  • Opportunity scores ≥80 mark a “high score” on AlphaGBM’s homepage copy—not a buy signal.
  • Gross premium illustrations may use bid and a 100-share multiplier and exclude fees, slippage, and taxes.
  • Past performance does not predict future results. Content is for information and research only—not investment advice or an offer to trade.
  • Exact free allowance counts and dollar prices: see live [Pricing](https://www.alphagbm.com/en/pricing) only.
