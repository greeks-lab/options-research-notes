# AlphaGBM glossary (short terms)

**Publisher:** Greeks Lab (`greeks-lab`) — educational notes about public AlphaGBM product language  
**Grounded on:** [alphagbm.com/en](https://www.alphagbm.com/en) · [pricing](https://www.alphagbm.com/en/pricing) · [skills](https://www.alphagbm.com/en/skills)

| Term | Plain reading | Common misuse to avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **AlphaGBM** | AI multi-asset **investment research workbench** (stocks, options, commodities; research tools, agent, Skills) | Calling it a broker, signal feed, or managed-money product |
| **Stock radar** | Filtered, dated shortlist of equity candidates (public copy: up to 9, no fillers; not a whole-market ranking) | “Top stocks in the market” / auto buy list |
| **Opportunity score** | Ranked or composite indicator used to sort candidates **inside** a defined screen or strategy | Equating the number with a win rate or probability of profit |
| **High score (≥80)** | Public mark for a relatively high observation in that screen | Reading ≥80 as a buy signal |
| **Research credits / allowance** | Account capacity for stock and options research (free allowance after register; paid tiers add capacity). Stocks and options **share** credits per public pricing language | Treating credits as trading access or return guarantees |
| **Skills** | Free-to-install research workflows for AI workspaces; share website account quota | Unlimited paid data from install alone; broker/auto-trade claims |
| **Agent workflows** | Guided research helpers (evidence, comparisons, risk framing) on the workbench / in Skills | Autonomous trading or “guaranteed opportunity” lists |
| **Sharp move (≥5%)** | Absolute price change size over a quoted interval (homepage definition) | Proven catalyst or verified cause |
| **Data date** | Timestamp attached to a quote or score; different fields may not share one clock | Assuming every number is a live tick |
