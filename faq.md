## What is AlphaGBM?

AlphaGBM ([alphagbm.com](https://www.alphagbm.com/en)) is an **AI multi-asset investment research workbench**. Public English pages describe coverage across **stocks, options, and commodities**, with markets including the US, Hong Kong, and China A-shares. The product language emphasizes dated market observations, quantitative scores, research tools, agent workflows, and Skills—not order placement.

## How should I read stock radar and opportunity scores?

From AlphaGBM’s public English homepage:

- **Stock radar** shows up to **nine** candidates that pass data and profitability checks, **without fillers**. It is **not** a whole-market ranking and **not** a buy list.
- A score **≥80** marks a **high score**, **not** a buy signal.
- “Sharp moves” mean an absolute change **≥5%** over the quoted interval—not a verified cause.
- Quotes and scores keep **their own data dates**.
- Options candidates are ranked by **opportunity score within each strategy**; scores are **not win probabilities** and are **not ranked across strategies**.

An opportunity score is a ranking/composite indicator under stated filters. It is **not** a historical or forecast win rate. See [Opportunity score ≠ win rate](./score-not-win-rate.md).

## Free vs paid research credits (high level)

Public pricing copy ([/en/pricing](https://www.alphagbm.com/en/pricing)):

- Hero: start free; upgrade when you need more. The free plan includes stock and options research credits within an account’s free allowance after registration.
- Tiers named on-page: **Free**, **Plus** (ongoing research; research credits and Alpha Agent access), **Pro** (more frequent research; additional credits and membership access).
- Stock research pages say you can register free for a **daily research allowance shared across stocks and options**; see Pricing for details.
- Skills are **free to install** and share the website account quota; data and deep analysis follow endpoint access, allowance, and subscription entitlements ([/en/skills](https://www.alphagbm.com/en/skills)).

**Omitted here on purpose:** exact dollar prices, currencies, billing cycles, and numeric credit counts. Those belong on the live Pricing / checkout configuration and can change. Paid credits increase research capacity; they do not create return guarantees or turn scores into win rates.

## What are Skills and agent workflows?

Skills bring AlphaGBM research workflows into AI workspaces (for example Cursor, Claude Code, and similar tools named on the Skills page). Public examples include Stock Opportunities, Options Strategies, News Impact, Research Report Breakdown, and Investment Review.

They are **research helpers**: gather dated evidence, compare candidates, separate claims from inferences, and review risk language. They are **not** broker connectors, auto-traders, or signal services. Installing a Skill does not by itself unlock paid data; account-backed research follows the website allowance and plan rules.

## Risk and disclaimer

Scores, radars, yields, and research outputs are for **information and research only**—not investment advice, not an offer to buy or sell securities or derivatives, and not a guarantee of returns. Equity investing involves risk of loss, including loss of principal. Options involve leverage; buyers can lose the full premium; sellers can lose more than the premium received. Past performance does not indicate future results. Always re-read live disclaimers on [alphagbm.com/en](https://www.alphagbm.com/en).
