## Short answer

AlphaGBM’s English pricing page invites users to **“Start Free. Upgrade When You Need More.”** After registration, the free plan includes stock and options research credits within an account’s **free allowance**. Plus and Pro are described as paid tiers for ongoing or more frequent research. Stocks and options **share** research credits. Public meta copy states that **Alpha Agent requires a paid subscription**. Exact dollar prices, currencies, billing cycles, and numeric credit counts should be taken **only** from the live checkout/configuration on [Pricing](https://www.alphagbm.com/en/pricing).

Paid credits increase **research capacity**. They do **not** create return guarantees, turn scores into win rates, or turn the workbench into a broker.
